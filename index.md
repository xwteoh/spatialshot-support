# Spatial Shot

Capture spatial photos and videos on iPhone, and relive them in 3D on Apple Vision Pro.

## Getting started

- **Which iPhones work:** Spatial Shot needs an iPhone whose two rear lenses, Wide and Ultra Wide, sit side by side in a line. It's designed for iPhone Duo. iPhones with one rear camera, or with lenses set diagonally, can't capture in 3D, and Spatial Shot tells you so.
- **Hold it the right way:** the two lenses must sit side by side, like your eyes. If they're stacked, Spatial Shot shows a reminder to turn your iPhone.
- **Best results:** stand 1–2 meters (3–6 feet) from your subject, hold steady and level, and shoot in good light. Spatial Shot tells you when something needs fixing, such as "Step back" when the subject is too close.

## Taking photos and videos

- **Photo or Video:** swipe across the viewfinder or tap the switch beside the shutter. The volume buttons take the shot too.
- **Focus:** tap your subject to focus there, like in Camera. The "Step back" check then measures the distance to what you tapped.
- **Video format:** in Video, tap the parts of the label at the top to change the shape (3:4 or 9:16 on iPhone Duo), the quality (HD or 4K), and the frame rate (30 or 60 fps).
- **HDR:** photos and videos are HDR by default, for brighter highlights on HDR screens and Apple Vision Pro. Turn off **HDR Photo** or **HDR Video** in Settings (the gear) if another device shows them poorly.
- **Automatic quality:** the Ultra Wide lens's eye is softer, so Spatial Shot sharpens it on your iPhone, and sets the depth of each capture from the capture itself.

## Viewing and sharing

Your captures are saved to Photos. Tap the thumbnail beside the shutter to see your captures in the app. Pinch or double-tap a photo to zoom in.

Tap **Share** to choose:

- **Spatial** — the 3D original, for Apple Vision Pro.
- **2D** — a regular photo or video, for anyone.
- **Side by Side** — both eyes next to each other, for 3D viewers and headsets.
- **Red-Cyan 3D** — for red-cyan 3D glasses.

**To see your captures in 3D on Apple Vision Pro:** AirDrop them from Spatial Shot's viewer, or turn on iCloud Photos on your iPhone and Vision Pro and open Photos there. On iPhone they appear as regular photos and videos; the depth is kept for Vision Pro.

## Common questions

**The 3D is uncomfortable or looks off on Vision Pro.**
Stand farther back (1–2 meters) and hold your iPhone level. Spatial Shot sets the depth of every capture automatically. If one still looks wrong, send it to us with Settings › Feedback.

**My captures don't appear in Spatial Shot's viewer.**
Check that Spatial Shot has Photos access (Settings › Apps › Spatial Shot › Photos). With Limited Access it shows the captures it makes. Captures that couldn't be saved are kept in the app and saved automatically next time it can.

**Recording stopped by itself.**
Spatial Shot stops and saves a recording if your iPhone gets too warm or storage runs low, so the video is never lost.

**How do I see the tips again?**
Tap the gear, then **Show Tips Again**.
