# Spatial Shot

Capture spatial photos and videos on iPhone, and relive them in 3D on Apple Vision Pro.

## Getting started

- **Which iPhones work:** Spatial Shot needs an iPhone with two rear cameras, Wide and Ultra Wide. It's designed for iPhone Duo. iPhones with only one rear camera can't capture spatial photos or videos.
- **Spatial video:** hold iPhone Duo upright, so its two lenses sit side by side like your eyes. Spatial Shot shows a reminder if the phone is turned the wrong way.
- **Best results:** stand 1–2 meters (3–6 feet) from your subject, hold steady and level, and shoot in good light. Spatial Shot tells you when something needs fixing.

## Viewing in 3D

Your captures are saved to Photos. Tap the thumbnail beside the shutter to review your recent captures in the app. To see them in 3D:

- **On Apple Vision Pro:** in Spatial Shot's viewer, tap Share and AirDrop the capture to your Vision Pro. Or turn on iCloud Photos on your iPhone and Vision Pro, then open Photos on Vision Pro.
- **On iPhone:** captures appear as regular photos and videos; the depth is kept and shows on Vision Pro.

## Common questions

**Depth looks inside-out on Vision Pro.**
Open **Pro** in Spatial Shot and turn on **Swap Eyes**, then capture again.

**A capture didn't appear in Photos.**
Check that Spatial Shot has Photos access (Settings › Apps › Spatial Shot › Photos). Captures that couldn't be saved are kept in the app and saved automatically next time you open it.

**Recording stopped by itself.**
Spatial Shot stops and saves a recording if iPhone gets too warm or storage runs low, so the video is never lost.

**How do I see the tips again?**
Open **Pro** and tap **Show Tips Again**.
