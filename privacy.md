# Spatial Shot Privacy Policy

_Effective October 25, 2026_

Spatial Shot is a camera app for capturing spatial photos and videos. It's built so that your photos, videos, and settings stay on your device.

## What Spatial Shot collects

**Nothing.** Spatial Shot doesn't collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

## Permissions Spatial Shot asks for

- **Camera** — to capture spatial photos and videos. Images are processed on your iPhone.
- **Microphone** — to record sound with spatial videos.
- **Photos (add only)** — to save your captures to your photo library. Spatial Shot can add photos and videos but can't see or read anything in your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Data stored on your iPhone

- **Your captures** are saved to your photo library. Spatial Shot also keeps its own copies of your 20 most recent captures on your iPhone, so you can review and share them in the app; iOS may remove these when storage runs low. If a capture can't be saved right away (for example, when Photos access is turned off), Spatial Shot keeps it on your iPhone and saves it to Photos the next time it can.
- **Your settings** (such as capture mode and Pro options) and whether you've seen the welcome screens and tips are stored on your iPhone.

Deleting Spatial Shot removes everything it stores. Captures already saved to your photo library stay there. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs your library under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children

Spatial Shot doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
