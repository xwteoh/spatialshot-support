# Spatial Shot Privacy Policy

_Effective October 30, 2026_

Spatial Shot is a camera app for capturing spatial photos and videos. It's built so that your photos, videos, and settings stay on your device.

## What Spatial Shot collects

**Nothing.** Spatial Shot doesn't collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code. Spatial Shot itself doesn't send anything over the internet.

All processing, including sharpening and depth adjustment, happens on your iPhone. When needed, iOS may download Apple's on-device image enhancement model, or download an original from iCloud Photos so you can view or share it; these are handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions Spatial Shot asks for

- **Camera** — to capture spatial photos and videos.
- **Microphone** — to record sound with spatial videos.
- **Photos** — to save your captures to your photo library and show them in Spatial Shot's viewer. With **Limited Access**, Spatial Shot only sees the captures it makes. When you choose **Open from Photos** in the viewer, Spatial Shot only receives the item you pick.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app.

## Data stored on your iPhone

- **Your captures** are saved to your photo library. Spatial Shot remembers which items in your library it captured, so its viewer can list them. If it can't read your library (for example, with add-only access), it keeps its own copies of your 20 most recent captures instead; iOS may remove these when storage runs low. If a capture can't be saved right away, Spatial Shot keeps it on your iPhone and saves it to Photos the next time it can.
- **Your settings** (such as capture mode, HDR, and video format), how many captures you've made, and whether you've seen the welcome screens and tips are stored on your iPhone.

Deleting Spatial Shot removes everything it stores. Captures already saved to your photo library stay there. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs your library under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Ratings and feedback

Spatial Shot may ask you to rate it, using Apple's standard rating prompt; ratings go to Apple. If you send feedback from Settings, it opens an email in your mail app with your app version, iPhone model, and iOS version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send the email. We use what you send only to reply and to improve Spatial Shot, and don't share it.

## Children

Spatial Shot doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
